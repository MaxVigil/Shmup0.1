## Known provenance

- The Product Owner provided and visually approved the complete five-sprite set for this project.
- The assets are AI-assisted raster artwork.
- Basic, Ranged, and Hunter were prepared as one human-made military UAV family.
- Elite Armoured and Elite Vulnerable form one alien/hybrid craft state pair.
- Runtime copies are mechanical downscales of the approved originals; no generative redraw was used during runtime optimization.

## Unrecovered evidence

The exact image-generator identity, session history, prompts, generation dates, and provider-side licence evidence could not be recovered after loss of the originating chats. This record must not invent or imply those facts.

## Use restriction

The files are approved for repository-local development, automated testing, product review, and local evaluation. They must not be included in an external playtest, public build, store submission, marketing release, or other external distribution until the Product Owner confirms that the applicable generator terms and rights evidence permit that use.
